esn-bots

esn-bots is a small, invite-only set of chat assistants (Telegram bots) used by ESN Turkey volunteers and their friends. It is a volunteer project, not an official ESN service, and it is not open to the public.

MaSecretary

MaSecretary lets you manage your own Google Calendar by writing normal sentences to a Telegram bot: add, list, move and delete events, find free time and get reminders. Each user connects their own Google account, and the bot only acts on that user's request.

Türkçe: esn-bots, ESN Türkiye gönüllüleri ve çevreleri için davetle kullanılan küçük bir Telegram asistanları projesidir; resmî bir ESN hizmeti değildir. MaSecretary, kendi Google Takvimini Telegram'dan normal cümlelerle yönetmeni sağlar.
